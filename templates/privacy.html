Who runs this site

{{ site_name }} is a student project run by {{ operator_name }}. It demonstrates a system that predicts whether insurance policyholders will renew their policies. For any question about this policy, write to {{ contact_email }}.

The data in the dashboard is synthetic

Every policyholder, policy, payment, prediction and campaign result in the dashboard is generated by scripts. None of it describes real people. Please do not enter real personal information about anyone else into this system.

What we collect about you

If you have an account, we store your name, your email address, your role (admin or agent) and your password. The password is stored only as a salted hash, so nobody, including the site owner, can read it.

When you log in, your browser keeps a login token in session storage. It is removed when you log out or close the tab. The site itself does not set cookies, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Server logs

Like most web servers, the hosting setup may record technical details of each request, such as your IP address, the time and the page requested. This is used to keep the service running and to investigate problems.

Third parties

The dashboard charts use a script called Chart.js, which your browser downloads from the jsDelivr content delivery network. jsDelivr can therefore see your IP address when you open the dashboard. The site is also run by a hosting provider, which handles the data described above on our behalf.

How we use your data

We use it to let you log in, to show the parts of the dashboard that match your role, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for marketing.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Account details are kept until you ask us to remove them. To see, correct or delete your account, email {{ contact_email }}.

Security

Passwords are hashed, login tokens are signed and expire after a limited time, and some actions are restricted to admin accounts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

This site is not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
